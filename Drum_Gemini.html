Interactive Drum Machine

Click pads to toggle sounds on specific beats, or press Play to start the loop.

How Music Rhythm Works

A rhythm is broken into beats. This machine uses an 8-step grid representing a 4/4 musical measure (divided into 8th notes). Beats 1, 3, 5, and 7 are the "downbeats", while 2, 4, 6, and 8 are the "upbeats".
