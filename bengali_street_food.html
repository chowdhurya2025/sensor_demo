Authentic Delicacies Showcase

A Visual & Interactive Celebration of Iconic Street Foods

Fuchka & Live Tok Fountain

Crispy hollow puris filled with spiced potato and chickpea mixture, drenched in continuous tamarind water (Tok).

Fuchka

Crispy fried spherical puri filled with spiced mashed potatoes, chickpeas, herbs, and served with sour and tangy tamarind water (tok).

Doi Fuchka

Fuchka shells topped with sweetened yogurt, spicy chutneys, sev, and chopped coriander for a creamy, tangy explosion.

Shingara

Flaky, golden-fried pastry stuffed with spiced potato filling, peanuts, and seasonal vegetables.

Shingara Chaat

Crushed hot shingaras topped with chickpea curry (ghugni), sweet yogurt, tamarind chutney, onions, and spicy chaat masala.

Chicken Samosa

Triangular crisp pockets generously packed with minced chicken cooked with aromatic spices and herbs.

Daal Puri

Soft yet crispy deep-fried flatbread stuffed with spiced yellow lentil (chana daal) paste.

Khichuri & Mejbani Beef

Rich rice-and-lentil bhuna khichuri served alongside slow-cooked Chittagong-style traditional spicy beef curry.

Chapli Kabaab with Puri

Spicy pan-fried flat beef patty infused with pomegranate seeds and herbs, paired with fluffy hot puris.

Steam Momos

Delicate steamed dumplings stuffed with juicy minced chicken/vegetables, served with spicy red chili chutney.

Jhol Momo

Steamed dumplings submerged in a bowl of warm, nutty, tangy, and spicy sesame-tomato broth (jhol).
