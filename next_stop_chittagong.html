Our Homemade Specials

Jhal Fuchka

Crispy hollow shells filled with our house-spiced potato & chickpea mix, finished with fiery homemade tamarind-mint water. One bite and you’ll understand why this is the stall favorite.

Doi Fuchka

Cool, creamy yogurt poured over crispy puris like a sweet-tangy fountain, loaded with spicy potato filling, sev and chaat masala. The ultimate balance of heat and cooling comfort — made fresh every day.

Shingara

Golden, flaky, homemade pyramids stuffed with spiced potato, cauliflower and peas. Served hot with our sharp tomato-chili chutney. Perfect with a cup of chai.

Shingara Chaat

Crushed shingara tossed with yogurt, tamarind, green chutney, onions, coriander and plenty of sev. Crunchy, creamy, tangy and spicy all at once — pure homemade street magic.

Chicken Samosa

Juicy homemade chicken filling seasoned with garam masala and green chili, wrapped in a shatteringly crisp pastry. Freshly fried and dangerously addictive.

Daal Puri

Soft, puffed puris stuffed with our spiced red lentil filling and deep-fried until golden. Tear one open and the fragrant homemade aroma hits you instantly. A true comfort classic.

Khichuri with Mejbani Gorur Mangsho

Soft, ghee-laced homemade khichuri paired with our special slow-cooked Mejbani-style beef. Tender, deeply spiced meat that melts into the rice — the heart of Chittagong hospitality, made right here in the USA.

Chapli Kabaab with Puri

Flat, crispy-edged, juicy homemade chapli patties loaded with coriander, tomato and green chili. Served sizzling with soft puris. Smoky, spicy and unforgettable.

Steam Momos

Delicate hand-pleated dumplings filled with juicy chicken or veggies, steamed until the wrappers turn translucent. Served with our fiery homemade tomato-chili achar.

Jhol Momo with Broth

Steamed momos swimming in our smoky, spicy sesame-tomato broth. Sip the tangy, nutty jhol between every dumpling — pure homemade comfort in a bowl.
